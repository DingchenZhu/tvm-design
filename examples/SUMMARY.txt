TVM Architecture (5 Layers):
  Layer 5: ML Frameworks (PyTorch, ONNX, TensorFlow)
  Layer 4: TVM Frontend (Import & convert to Relay IR)
  Layer 3: Relay IR (High-level graph optimization)
  Layer 2: TIR (Low-level tensor operations)
  Layer 1: Code Generation (Target-specific)
  Layer 0: Hardware Execution

PyTorch → TVM Flow:
  PyTorch Model
       ↓ torch.jit.trace()
  TorchScript Graph
       ↓ relay.frontend.from_pytorch()
  Relay IR
       ↓ Optimization passes
  Optimized Relay IR
       ↓ Your codegen
  VIS VPU Instructions

Operator Conversion:
  TorchScript: aten::pad(%x, [1,1,1,1], "constant", 0)
       ↓
  Handler: GraphImporter.pad_generic()
       ↓
  Relay IR: nn.pad(%x, [[1,1],[1,1]], pad_value=0)
